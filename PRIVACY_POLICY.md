# Privacy Policy for Notes App

**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

Welcome to **Notes** ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

* **Account Information:** When you sign in (e.g. via email or Google Account), we store your email address and an authentication token to sync your notes to your account.
* **User Content (Notes & Images):** We process and store the text, titles, checklists, formatting, and images that you explicitly create and upload within the Notes app.
* **Device & Diagnostic Data:** Minimal network error logs to diagnose synchronization failures and improve app stability. We do not track location or personal device identifiers for advertising purposes.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

* **To Provide Core Services:** To display, edit, store, and synchronize your notes across your devices.
* **To Secure Your Account:** To authenticate your access so only you can read or edit your notes.
* **To Provide Customer Support:** To address inquiries or feedback sent via our official support channels.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security

* **Local Storage:** Your notes and images are cached locally on your device using encrypted/sandboxed app storage.
* **Cloud Sync:** Data transmitted between your device and the cloud backend is encrypted using industry-standard **HTTPS (TLS 1.3)**.
* **Cloudflare Infrastructure:** Synced notes and media are stored on Cloudflare D1/R2 secure cloud infrastructure.
* We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or brokers.

---

### 4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We only share data with essential infrastructure service providers necessary to operate the service:
* **Cloudflare Workers / R2 / D1:** Cloud hosting and secure data synchronization.
* **Google Identity Services:** If you choose to log in using Google Single Sign-On (SSO).

We do not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs or tracking pixels.

---

### 5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

* **Deleting Notes:** Deleting a note in the app removes it immediately or places it in Trash. Emptying Trash deletes the note and associated images permanently.
* **Account Deletion:** You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated cloud data at any time by contacting our support team.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

The Notes app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
